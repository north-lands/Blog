How this is counted

计算规则

Which Bilt option 取哪种算法

  • Under the current rules, the calculator works out both Bilt options below and uses the higher one.现行规则下，系统把下面两种 Bilt 算法都算一遍，取更高的那种。
  • Under the 2027 rules, only housing points are left.2027 新规则下只剩住房积分这一种。

Housing points 住房积分

  • Everyday spend on the Bilt card is compared with the housing amount.用 Bilt 卡上的日常消费和住房金额比。
  • Reaching 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100% of housing earns 0.5×, 0.75×, 1×, or 1.25× on housing. Below 25% still earns 250 points a month.达到住房的 25%、50%、75%、100%，住房分别给 0.5、0.75、1、1.25 倍。不到 25% 每月保底 250 分。
  • These are steps. Short of the next step, you stay on the lower one: 24% gets only the 250 points, 49% gets 0.5×, 99% gets 1×.档位是台阶式的，没到下一档就停在下面一档，不会归零。比如 24% 只有保底，49% 按 0.5 倍，99% 按 1 倍。
  • The share is counted on the full year. Bilt checks each month, so in real life a slow month can drop a step.比例按全年合计算。Bilt 实际每个月单独看，所以现实中某个月花得少可能会掉档。
  • With more than one card, the calculator may move some spend onto Bilt to reach the next step, when that pays more.选了多张卡时，如果更划算，系统会把其他卡的一部分消费挪到 Bilt 上，凑到下一档。
  • This option earns no Bilt Cash.这条路不产生 Bilt Cash。

Bilt Cash, current rules Bilt Cash · 现行规则

  • Spend on the Bilt card earns 4% Bilt Cash. Palladium’s included $200 goes into the same pool; it is not a credit you type in.Bilt 卡上的消费按 4% 产生 Bilt Cash。Palladium 附带的 $200 也进同一个池子，不在额度里另填。
  • First, it unlocks housing points: $30 becomes 1,000 points, up to 1× of the housing amount.先换住房积分：每 $30 换 1,000 分，最多把住房做到 1 倍。
  • Next, Obsidian and Palladium buy the Point Accelerator with what is left: $200 becomes 5,000 points, at most 5 times a year, and each time needs $5,000 spent on Bilt. Blue has no accelerator.然后 Obsidian 和 Palladium 用剩下的买积分加速器：每 $200 换 5,000 分，一年最多 5 次，每次要在 Bilt 上消费满 $5,000。Blue 没有加速器。
  • Cash still left counts as $0.再剩下的记 $0。

2027 rules 2027 新规则

  • Who keeps the current rules: a card opened by December 31, 2026 that opts out of the new benefits before its first card anniversary. New cards from 2027 get the new rules.谁能保留现行规则：2026 年 12 月 31 日前开卡，并在首个开卡周年日前选择保留旧权益。2027 年起的新卡用新规则。
  • Everyday spend no longer earns 4% Bilt Cash. Housing earns only through the housing-points steps above.日常消费不再返 4% Bilt Cash，住房只按上面住房积分的档位给分。
  • Bilt Cash can no longer unlock housing points. Here it only buys the Point Accelerator: each $200 adds +1× on the next $5,000 of Bilt spend, up to 5 times a year.Bilt Cash 不能再换住房积分，这里只拿来买积分加速器：每 $200 让接下来 $5,000 的 Bilt 消费多 1 倍，一年最多 5 次。
  • Where it comes from: Palladium’s $600 a year, plus $50 for every 25,000 Bilt points earned (everyday and housing points).来源：Palladium 每年送的 $600，加上每累计 25,000 分（日常和住房积分）送的 $50。
  • Obsidian’s $100 a year is not enough for one accelerator, so it is a credit whose worth you type in.Obsidian 每年的 $100 不够开一次加速器，所以当作额度，由你填实际能用掉的金额。
  • Cash still left counts as $0. Daily Rewards are not counted.再剩下的记 $0。Daily Rewards 不计入。

Citi Custom Cash

  • Each month it picks the eligible category with the largest added reward. The first $500 there earns 5× TYP; the rest goes to the best selected card.每月从合资格类别里选增量收益最高的一类，前 $500 按 5 倍 TYP，剩下的交给已选卡里回报最高的一张。
  • Eligible here: dining, grocery, gas, and travel. Online shopping and the mixed “other” bucket are not.本计算器把餐饮、超市、加油、旅行算作合资格；网购和混合的「其他」不算。
  • The same spending cannot also use Freedom Flex 5×.同一笔消费不能再叠加 Freedom Flex 的 5 倍。

Atmos Rewards Ascent

  • Only rent earns, not mortgage. The card is hidden when Mortgage is picked.只有房租有奖励，房贷没有。选房贷时这张卡不显示。
  • Rent paid through Bilt costs a 3% fee and earns 3× Atmos on the charged amount, up to $50,000 a year (about $4,167 a month).房租经 Bilt 刷这张卡要付 3% 手续费，按刷卡金额给 3 倍 Atmos，每年上限 $50,000（约每月 $4,167）。
  • Above $50,000 it earns only 1× and the fee still applies, so it pays only when Atmos is worth more than about 2.91¢. Otherwise that part stays off the card and counts as $0.超过 $50,000 的部分只有 1 倍，手续费照收，只有 Atmos 估值高于约 2.91 美分才划算；否则这部分不刷这张卡，记 $0。

More than one of the same card 同一张卡有几张

  • Custom Cash, Freedom Flex, and Customized Cash can be counted 1 to 3 times.Custom Cash、Freedom Flex、Customized Cash 可以填 1 到 3 张。
  • Each Custom Cash picks its own best category with its own $500 a month, one after another. Each Freedom Flex or Customized Cash adds another full cap.每张 Custom Cash 各自挑一个最划算的类别，各有每月 $500，依次分配；每张 Freedom Flex 或 Customized Cash 多一份完整额度。
  • The same spending is still counted only once.同一笔消费仍然只算一次。

Fees and credits 年费和额度

  • Every selected card’s fee is charged.选中的每张卡都扣年费。
  • Credits count only the amount you type.额度只算你填进去的金额。

Keep in mind 别忘了

These numbers are only the math. They do not price the extra work. Bilt takes more attention than a plain cash-back card: credits have to be used, and Bilt Cash has to be spent or it is worth nothing here. A smaller bank is also less of a sure thing than Chase or Citi. Time is money too.

以上只按数学计算，没有把费心算进去。Bilt 比一张普通返现卡更要盯着：各种额度要记得用掉，Bilt Cash 也要花完，否则这里记成 0。小银行也不如大银行稳妥。时间也是钱。

Bilt Rules

Bilt 规则

This switch only changes how the Bilt cards (Blue, Obsidian, Palladium) are counted. Other cards are not affected.

这个开关只影响 Bilt 卡（Blue、Obsidian、Palladium）的算法，其他卡不受影响。

现行规则：2026 年底前开卡、并在首个开卡周年日前选择保留旧权益的老用户，可以继续用 4% Bilt Cash。
