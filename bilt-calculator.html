How this is counted

计算规则

The higher of the two Bilt options is used.

两种 Bilt 算法里，取更高的一种。

Housing points. Spend on the Bilt card is compared with housing. Below 25% of housing, it still earns 250 points a month. The housing rate is then 0.5×, 0.75×, 1×, and 1.25× when that spend reaches 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of housing. This option does not earn Bilt Cash.

住房积分。用 Bilt 上的日常消费和住房比。不到住房的 25%，每月仍有 250 分。消费达到住房的 25%、50%、75%、100% 时，住房分别是 0.5 倍、0.75 倍、1 倍、1.25 倍。这条路不产生 Bilt Cash。

Bilt Cash. Spend on the Bilt card earns 4% Bilt Cash. Palladium also adds its included $200 to that same pool. It is not a credit you type in. The pool is used in this order. First it unlocks housing points: $30 becomes 1,000 points, stopping at 1× of the housing amount. Next, on Obsidian and Palladium, what is left buys the Point Accelerator: $200 becomes 5,000 points, at most 5 times a year, and each time needs $5,000 spent on Bilt. Blue has no accelerator. Cash still left is $0.

Bilt Cash。Bilt 上的消费按 4% 产生 Bilt Cash。Palladium 附带的 $200 也加进同一个池子，不在额度里另填。池子按这个顺序花：先换住房积分，每 $30 换 1,000 分，最多把住房做到 1 倍。然后 Obsidian 和 Palladium 用剩下的买积分加速器，每 $200 换 5,000 分，一年最多 5 次，每次还要在 Bilt 上消费满 $5,000。Blue 没有加速器。再剩下的记 $0。

Citi Custom Cash. Each month, the calculator chooses the eligible category with the largest added reward. The first $500 in that category earns 5× TYP; the rest goes to the best selected card. Dining, grocery, gas, and travel are eligible here. Online shopping and the mixed “other” bucket are not. The same spending cannot also use Freedom Flex 5×.

Citi Custom Cash。系统每月从合资格类别里选择增量收益最高的一类，前 $500 按 5 倍 TYP，剩余消费交给已选卡里回报最高的一张。本计算器把餐饮、超市、加油、旅行列为合资格；网购和混合的「其他」不算。同一笔消费不能再叠加 Freedom Flex 的 5 倍。

More than one of the same card. Custom Cash, Freedom Flex, and Customized Cash can be counted 1 to 3 times. Each Custom Cash picks its own best category with its own $500 a month, one after another. Each Freedom Flex or Customized Cash adds another full cap. The same spending still cannot be counted twice.

同一张卡有几张。Custom Cash、Freedom Flex、Customized Cash 可以填 1 到 3 张。每张 Custom Cash 各自挑一个最划算的类别，各有每月 $500，依次分配；每张 Freedom Flex 或 Customized Cash 多一份完整额度。同一笔消费仍然只算一次。

Every selected card’s fee is charged. Credits count only the amount you type.

选中的每张卡都扣年费。额度只算你填进去的金额。

These numbers are only the math. They do not price the extra work. Bilt takes more attention than a plain cash-back card: credits have to be used, and Bilt Cash has to be spent or it is worth nothing here. A smaller bank is also less of a sure thing than Chase or Citi. Time is money too.

以上只按数学计算，没有把费心算进去。Bilt 比一张普通返现卡更要盯着：各种额度要记得用掉，Bilt Cash 也要花完，否则这里记成 0。小银行也不如大银行稳妥。时间也是钱。
